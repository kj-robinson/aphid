Below is the metadata for the experimental data files associated with [...]

#### **aphid.csv**

\- `cage`: unique identity of cage used in experiment (U: unwarmed or
W: warmed, N: no predator or P: predator, followed by replicate number 1-10)

\- `count`: survey number from 0-14

\- `date`: date of survey (YYYY-MM-DD)

\- `warmingtreatment`: presence or absence of warming (unwarmed:
unwarmed/ambient treatment, warmed: warmed treatment)

\- `predatortreatment`: presence or absence of predators (nopredator:
no-predator treatment, predator: predator treatment)

\- `sticky_wingless`: number of aphids on the sticky card that do not have
wings

\- `sticky_winged`: number of aphids on the sticky card that have wings

\- `sentinel_wingless`: number of aphids on the sentinel plant that do not
have wings

\- `sentinel_winged`: number of aphids on the sentinel plant that have wings

\- `focal_wingless`: number of aphids on the focal plant that do not have wings

\- `focal_winged`: number of aphids on the focal plant that have wings

#### **temperature.csv**

\- `cage`: unique identity of cage used in experiment (U: unwarmed or
W: warmed, N: no predator or P: predator, followed by replicate number 1-10)

\- `warmingtreatment`: presence or absence of warming (unwarmed:
unwarmed/ambient treatment, warmed: warmed treatment)

\- `predatortreatment`: presence or absence of predators (nopredator:
no-predator treatment, predator: predator treatment)

\- `datetime`: date and time of temperature reading by logger
(MM-DD-YYYY HH:MM:SS)

\- `temperature`: temperature recorded in the cage by logger (degrees Celsius)

#### **plant.csv**

\- `cage`: unique identity of cage used in experiment (U: unwarmed or
W: warmed, N: no predator or P: predator, followed by replicate number 1-10)

\- `warmingtreatment`: presence or absence of warming (unwarmed:
unwarmed/ambient treatment, warmed: warmed treatment)

\- `predatortreatment`: presence or absence of predators (nopredator:
no-predator treatment, predator: predator treatment)

\- `period`: time period of data collection (start: beginning of the growing
season, end: end of the growing season)

\- `leaves`: number of live leaves on focal plant
